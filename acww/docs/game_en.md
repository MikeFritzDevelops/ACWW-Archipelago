# Animal Crossing: Wild World

## Goal

Complete the museum by donating the required exhibits and finish the selected museum goal percentage (or 100%, depending on settings).

Victory is awarded automatically after reaching the configured museum completion goal.

---

## Progression

Your progression revolves around collecting items from Archipelago.

### Base Tools

The following tools are progression items:

- Shovel
- Fishing Rod
- Net

If Starter Kit is enabled, these are received during the tutorial.
Otherwise, they must be found through Archipelago.

### Golden Tools

Golden tools are optional utility upgrades.

They do **not** replace the base tools in logic and are not required to complete the game, but provide their normal in-game benefits.

- Golden Shovel
- Golden Fishing Rod
- Golden Net
- Golden Axe
- Golden Watering Can
- Golden Slingshot

---

## Museum Specimens

When Museumsanity is enabled, bugs, fish, fossils, and paintings become Archipelago items.

Receiving a specimen unlocks the ability to donate it to the museum.

Catchsanity checks still require catching the creature yourself.

---

## Month Progression

Only one month is available at the start.

The remaining months are unlocked by finding Month items in Archipelago.

This controls which seasonal bugs and fish become available.

---

## Checks

Depending on settings, checks can include:

- Catching bugs
- Catching fish
- Museum donations
- Museum completion milestones
- Journal milestones
- Fossil exhibits
- Paintings
- Finding a Four-leaf clover from clover weeds.
- Tutorial rewards

---

## Notes

- Bells are filler.
- Clothing and furniture are filler.
- Golden tools are useful items.
- Base tools are progression.
